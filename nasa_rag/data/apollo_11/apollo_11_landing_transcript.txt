The landing was notable for the program alarms 1201 and 1202 raised by the Lunar Module's guidance computer during the final descent, indicating executive overflow. Steve Bales, the guidance officer, and his back room engineers determined the alarms were not critical and gave the call to continue the landing. Armstrong manually piloted the Lunar Module past a boulder-strewn area, landing with approximately 25 seconds of fuel remaining.
