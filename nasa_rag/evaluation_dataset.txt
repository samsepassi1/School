Q: Give me a high-level overview of the Apollo 11 mission.
Mission: Apollo 11
Category: overview
Expected: Apollo 11 was the first crewed lunar landing, launched July 16, 1969 from Kennedy Space Center on a Saturn V. Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin landed in the Lunar Module Eagle at Tranquility Base on July 20, 1969; Michael Collins remained in lunar orbit aboard Columbia. The crew returned with 21.55 kg of lunar samples and splashed down in the Pacific on July 24, 1969.

Q: What problems did Apollo 13 encounter and how was the crew brought home safely?
Mission: Apollo 13
Category: emergency
Expected: At ~55 hours 55 minutes mission elapsed time an electrical short ignited oxygen tank 2, which ruptured and damaged tank 1, knocking out the fuel cells. The crew used the Lunar Module Aquarius as a lifeboat, executed a PC+2 burn with the LM descent engine, jury-rigged a CO2 scrubber adapter from plastic bag, cardboard, suit hoses, and duct tape, and re-entered safely on April 17, 1970.

Q: What was the technical root cause of the Challenger disaster and which factors contributed?
Mission: Challenger
Category: disaster analysis
Expected: The Rogers Commission concluded the right SRB joint's primary and secondary O-rings failed at the cold launch temperature (~36F), allowing hot gases to breach the joint, impinge on the external tank, and trigger aerodynamic breakup. Contributing factors included a temperature-sensitive O-ring design, Morton Thiokol engineers' overridden no-launch recommendation, internal communication failures, and schedule pressure.

Q: What were the 1201 and 1202 program alarms during the Apollo 11 landing?
Mission: Apollo 11
Category: technical
Expected: They were executive-overflow alarms raised by the Lunar Module's guidance computer during final descent. Guidance officer Steve Bales and his back-room engineers determined the alarms were not critical and cleared Eagle to continue. Armstrong then piloted past a boulder field, landing with about 25 seconds of fuel remaining.

Q: Give me the key timeline of Apollo 11 from launch to splashdown.
Mission: Apollo 11
Category: timeline
Expected: Launch July 16, 1969 13:32 UTC from LC-39A; lunar orbit insertion July 19 17:21 UTC; Eagle landing July 20 20:17 UTC at Tranquility Base; Armstrong's first step July 21 02:56 UTC; Pacific splashdown July 24 16:50 UTC.

Q: What was the first exchange between Apollo 13 and Mission Control after the oxygen tank explosion?
Mission: Apollo 13
Category: timeline
Expected: At about 55h 55m MET Jack Swigert reported "Okay, Houston, we've had a problem here." CAPCOM Jack Lousma asked him to repeat, and Jim Lovell radioed "Houston, we've had a problem. We've had a main B bus undervolt." Within minutes Lovell also reported "It looks to me, looking out the hatch, that we are venting something out into space."
