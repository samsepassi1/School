PROJECT SUMMARY AND CONCLUSIONS

Dataset: Amazon Reviews (Beauty & Personal Care category)
Sample Size: 100 reviews + 100 metadata items

--- Key Findings ---

1. Rating Distribution:
   - Reviews are fairly evenly distributed across 1-5 star ratings
   - Mean rating: ~3.5 stars
   - The histogram showed no strong skew, suggesting honest customer feedback

2. Price vs. Rating:
   - No strong linear correlation between product price and average rating
   - Products at all price points received both high and low ratings
   - This challenges the assumption that higher-priced products are better-rated

3. Missing Data Handling:
   - ~24% of metadata items lacked price information
   - Resolved with median imputation (median price: ~$48.67)
   - try-except blocks safeguarded against KeyError and missing field access

4. Verified vs. Unverified Purchases:
   - ~50% of reviews were from verified purchases
   - Important context for weighting review credibility

--- Most Helpful Analysis Method ---
The combined subplot visualization (Part 6.3) was the most impactful — it allowed
simultaneous comparison of the rating distribution and price relationship, which is
exactly the kind of at-a-glance insight useful for business presentations.

The try-except exception handling (Part 3.3) was the most important for production
readiness — large datasets from Hugging Face frequently have incomplete records, and
robust error handling prevents crashes and surfaces data quality issues cleanly.
