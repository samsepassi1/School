Product Specification: Email Router

Project: Email Router (Pilot)
Owner: InnovateNext Solutions
Audience: Technical Project Managers, Engineering Leadership, Customer Success

1. Overview
The Email Router is a SaaS service that ingests inbound email for a customer
domain and routes each message to the correct internal team mailbox (for
example: support, sales, billing, engineering, abuse). Routing decisions are
produced by a hybrid pipeline that combines deterministic rules (regex,
sender domain, headers) with an LLM-based classifier that interprets message
intent. Customers manage rules, mailboxes, and review escalations from a
web-based admin dashboard.

2. Goals
- Reduce time-to-first-response on inbound email by routing within seconds.
- Eliminate manual triage for 80%+ of inbound messages on the Standard tier.
- Provide auditability so every routing decision can be explained and
  overridden.
- Be safe to deploy: no message is ever lost, and misrouted messages can be
  re-routed in one click.

3. Personas
- Admin: configures the organisation, connects mailboxes, manages billing,
  and invites teammates.
- Team Lead: owns one or more team mailboxes (e.g. Support Lead, Sales Lead);
  defines routing rules for their team and reviews escalations.
- Agent: a frontline teammate who reads and replies to messages routed to
  their mailbox.
- Auditor: a compliance role that needs read-only access to routing logs,
  decisions, and overrides.
- End User: the external person who sent the email; they never log in but
  must always receive a timely, on-topic response.

4. Functional Requirements
4.1 Email Ingestion
- Connect to customer mail providers via OAuth: Google Workspace and
  Microsoft 365 at minimum.
- Support a generic IMAP fallback for self-hosted mail.
- Ingest new messages within 30 seconds of arrival.
- Preserve original message headers and attachments.

4.2 Routing Engine
- Evaluate deterministic rules first (priority-ordered list).
- If no rule matches with high confidence, invoke the LLM-based classifier
  with the message subject, sender, and a redacted body.
- Output a routing decision: target mailbox, confidence score, and a short
  rationale.
- Confidence threshold for auto-route is configurable per organisation.
- Below threshold, the message is sent to a human review queue.

4.3 Admin Dashboard
- Sign-in with email + password and SSO (Google, Microsoft, SAML on
  Enterprise).
- Mailbox connections page (add, test, remove).
- Rules editor: create, reorder, enable/disable rules; rules support sender,
  subject regex, body keywords, and header conditions.
- Review queue: shows messages below the confidence threshold; team leads
  can approve, reroute, or send to another mailbox.
- Audit log: every routing decision and override is recorded with actor,
  timestamp, original mailbox, target mailbox, and rationale.

4.4 Notifications
- Real-time notifications to a target mailbox when a message is routed there.
- Daily digest summarising volume, confidence distribution, and overrides.
- Slack and Microsoft Teams integrations for the digest.

4.5 Reporting
- Volume by team, by day, by source domain.
- Override rate (a leading indicator of model quality).
- Average time from ingestion to delivery.
- Export to CSV.

5. Non-Functional Requirements
- Availability: 99.9% on Standard, 99.95% on Enterprise.
- Latency: p95 routing decision under 5 seconds end-to-end.
- Security: data encrypted in transit and at rest; PII redaction before any
  prompt is sent to the LLM provider.
- Compliance: SOC 2 Type II within 12 months of launch.
- Data residency: US and EU regions at launch.

6. Out of Scope (for the pilot)
- Outbound email sending or auto-reply drafting.
- Voice or chat channels.
- On-premises deployment.

7. Pricing Tiers
- Standard: $29 / seat / month. Up to 3 connected mailboxes per seat.
- Enterprise: $79 / seat / month. SSO, audit log retention 2 years, 99.95%
  SLA, priority support.

8. Success Metrics
- Time-to-first-response reduced by 40% within 60 days of go-live.
- Auto-route share above 80% on the Standard tier.
- Override rate below 5%.
- Customer NPS above 40 at the end of the pilot.
